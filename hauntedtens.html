Haunted Tens

tens and ones in the Pumpkin King's cellar

Even the Pumpkin King had to learn his tens! In my cellar the pumpkins come one by one or packed in tens. Pick a room, watch, then try it yourself. Every room you finish lights a lantern.

For grown-ups

This is the explainer behind Haunted House's Grade 1 tens questions — teen numbers, tens and ones, 62 + 10, adding and taking away tens, adding ones and making a new ten, comparing, the hundred square and counting to 120. It does not ask quiz questions from a bank: each room shows the idea with pumpkins you can move, says it aloud, and then asks the child to do it — build a number, press + ten, bundle ten ones, move the bat on the hundred square.

A wrong try gets a hint, never the answer (the same rule as Haunted House). After three tries a Skip appears. The explanation comes after the child gets it. Rooms run in the suggested order but every room is open. Nothing leaves this device; progress is kept in this browser only.

A nicer voice: the voice menu at the top picks the best natural voice the device has. The best ones (★★ Premium, ★ Enhanced) must be downloaded once — on an iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak (or Spoken Content) → Voices → English, then download Ava (Premium) or Samantha (Enhanced), and reload this page. On a Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → Manage Voices.
